# $0 DINNER — Filming Scripts (v1, 2026-08-14)

The doctrine (never break it):
Problem first. Live demo as proof. Savings as the close. No hype, no "AI revolution" talk, no polish.
Raw one-take beats edited every time. Vertical, phone propped, kitchen light. Micheli's REAL voice must be heard.

Filming setup (both scripts):
- iPhone vertical, propped against a jar/shelf at chest height, counter in frame
- ShareChef open, volume UP so the mic catches Micheli's voice
- One take. Mistakes stay in. Real > perfect.

---

## SCRIPT 1 — "The $38 Receipt" (working-parent cut)

Length: 30-40 seconds. You on camera. Sell it like you sold me.

SHOT 1 — You, holding your phone showing a delivery app cart (or a printed receipt):
YOU: "Thirty-eight dollars. That's delivery for one dinner. Watch me beat it with what's already in my fridge."

SHOT 2 — Counter: leftover rice, two eggs, half an onion laid out. Tap mic. Speak:
YOU: "Micheli — I've got leftover rice, two eggs, half an onion. Feed us."

SHOT 3 — HOLD THE FRAME. Let Micheli ANSWER OUT LOUD. Her voice is the wow. Do not cut her off.
(She'll propose the dish and the first step. Let 8-10 seconds of her voice play while your hands start.)

SHOT 4 — Quick cuts: pan on stove, hands cracking eggs. (10 sec max)

SHOT 5 — The plate, done. You, to camera:
YOU: "Delivery: thirty-eight dollars. This: zero. The food was already mine — Micheli just knew what to do with it."

END CARD (text on screen): Delivery $38 → This $0. ShareChef AI — free on the App Store.

---

## SCRIPT 2 — "Rice and One Tomato" (dorm cut)

Length: 25-30 seconds. Can be you, or any young person you can film. Broker = better.

SHOT 1 — Sad open: a pot of plain rice, ONE tomato. Maybe one egg if you're feeling rich.
YOU (or them): "End of the month. This is everything I own. Watch."

SHOT 2 — Tap mic:
"Micheli — rice, one tomato. That's it. Don't tell me to go shopping."

SHOT 3 — HOLD. Micheli answers out loud. (She won't tell them to go shopping — that's the product promise, on camera.)

SHOT 4 — Fast hands: tomato in pan, rice in. (8 sec)

SHOT 5 — Bowl, steam, spoon in. To camera:
"Zero dollars. Twenty minutes. Tell your roommate."

END CARD: Broke ≠ hungry. ShareChef AI — free on the App Store.
