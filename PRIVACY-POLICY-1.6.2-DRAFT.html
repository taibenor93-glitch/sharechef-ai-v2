ShareChef Privacy Policy

Last updated: [SET AT PUBLISH]

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and login credentials, stored securely with Supabase. Recipes you save and content you share are stored with your account. If you use voice features, your speech is transmitted to OpenAI to generate Micheli's responses; ShareChef does not permanently store your voice recordings.

How we use your data

Your data is used only to provide ShareChef's features: generating recipes, powering voice conversations, saving your collection, and community sharing. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Subscriptions and payments

ShareChef Plus is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple ID, and the subscription renews each month until you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can view, change, or cancel it at any time in your App Store account settings.

ShareChef never sees or stores your payment details. Apple handles the transaction and does not share your card or billing information with us. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active; for that purpose RevenueCat receives your ShareChef account ID and the purchase record Apple issues, and returns only whether your subscription is currently valid. All we store on our side is which plan your account is on, so the app knows whether to apply the free-plan limit. Cancelling your subscription is done with Apple; deleting your ShareChef account does not cancel it.

Third-party services

ShareChef uses Supabase (accounts and data storage), OpenAI (recipe generation and voice processing), Railway (application hosting), and Apple together with RevenueCat (subscription purchases and their validation). Each processes data only as needed to operate the app.

Microphone access

ShareChef requests microphone access solely so you can talk to Micheli. Audio is processed in real time and is not used for any other purpose.

Data deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app: open Preferences, choose "Delete account", and confirm with your password. Deletion is immediate and irreversible — it removes your account, saved recipes, Micheli's memory of you, cooking sessions, and shares. Deleting your account does not cancel an active ShareChef Plus subscription; cancel that separately in your App Store account settings. If you cannot access the app, contact us at taibenor93@gmail.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

Children

ShareChef is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page.
