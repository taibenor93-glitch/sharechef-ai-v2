## Content law (every video)
Ingredients in second 1 → pain in big text → ShareChef's meal by second 5 →
one guided-cooking moment → finished dish by second 12 → one CTA.
No transformation, no post. Never lead with "I built an AI app" — nobody
exhausted craves AI; they crave dinner. Original footage, no watermarks,
same file to TikTok + IG Reels + FB Reels (one engine, three outlets).

### Video template
Opening text: "I almost spent $38 on delivery because I couldn't figure out what to make with this."
VO: "I had [3 ingredients]. Plenty of food, zero ideas. I gave them to ShareChef —
it created this dinner and Micheli guided me through it hands-free, one step at a
time. Comment three ingredients from your kitchen and I'll show you what she makes."
End card: "Stop paying twice for dinner. ShareChef AI — free on iPhone."

## WEEK 1 — Fridge Rescue loop (Aug 21–27) · target 120
Daily: film one real fridge/pantry rescue per the law above; publish to all three;
reply to every legit comment within the hour; turn 3 ingredient comments into
personalized replies; log 24h numbers (views, profile visits, store visits, downloads).
Kill rule: hook under 5 profile visits per 1,000 views after two tests dies.
Concepts — D1: three sad leftovers · D2: "too tired to decide" · D3: end-of-paycheck
dinner · D4: working parent + hungry teenager · D5: two ingredients + pantry staples ·
D6: delivery total vs. food already paid for · D7: viewer-submitted fridge challenge.
D5 SWAP (Tai's idea 08-21): the multilingual hook — Micheli switches language
mid-cook ("My mother only speaks Spanish. Her AI chef doesn't care."). English
framing, one language-switch moment on camera. Unique — nobody else can show this.
Rule stands: ONE video per day, English-led, until Week 1 data says otherwise.

## WEEK 3 — Reddit problem interception (Sep 1–7) · target 250 (cum. 720)
Daily video continues (reuse structure, never execution). On top, daily:
find 5 fresh "what can I make with…"/dinner-fatigue posts; write 5 genuinely
useful answers (usable meal suggestion in each, no link); 2 personalized recipe
rescues from posters' actual ingredients; disclose you built ShareChef when
relevant; ask mods before any dedicated post; max ONE promo post/day; never
identical wording across subs; answer every reply; log REDDIT-link downloads;
save recurring pain-phrases as future hooks.
Kill rule: 20 helpful comments with zero profile visits/app asks = comments too
generic — stop mentioning the app, get unusually specific, reintroduce only when
people ask how you generated the ideas.
Communities: frugal/budget cooking, cooking-for-one, beginner cooking, leftovers,
working parents, food waste, ADHD/executive function. Read each sub's rules first.

## WEEK 4 — Micro-creators (Sep 8–14) · target 220 (cum. 940)
2,000–30,000 followers in: budget meals, working-parent life, ADHD/executive
function, cooking-for-one, college meals, low-waste, beginner cooking, post-work
wellness. Relevance and comment quality over follower count.
Daily: identify 15 relevant creators; 3 real comments on priority creators'
posts before pitching; 10 personalized DMs; 5 follow-ups; offer the custom
three-ingredient Fridge Rescue (their ingredients → their episode); 1 free demo
for the best prospect; unique tracked link per creator; repost permission;
drop anyone wanting payment, faking engagement, or refusing attribution.
Weekly floor: 70 pitches, 25 follow-ups, 7 demos, 5 published collabs.
Kill rule: after 30 good DMs — <3 replies = weak pitch; replies but no collabs =
offer takes too much work; collabs but no downloads = wrong audience or weak CTA.

## DAYS 29–30 — Exploit the winner (Sep 15–19) · target 60 (total 1,000)
No fifth strategy. Rank channels by downloads/hour; kill the bottom two.
Recreate the winning hook with three new ingredient sets. Recontact everyone who
engaged but never clicked. Publish the honest 30-day results post with direct CTA.
Referral ask to active users: "Send ShareChef to one person who reaches 7 PM
with zero energy left to decide what to cook."

## Kill rules (sprint level)
- <120 by Aug 27: the demo or the App Store page is failing — fix message before volume.
- <470 by Sep 3: PH didn't carry it — Reddit starts immediately at 10 useful comments/day.
- <720 by Sep 10: creator DMs double to 20/day.
- Views without downloads = entertainment, not necessity. Store visits without
  downloads = the store page is the leak. Downloads without first recipes
  completed = curiosity, not users.

## Anti-advice traps (never do)
Quote cards, brand films, font-picking, "cutting-edge AI" talk, identical
reposts, watermark reuploads, link-dropping under viral videos, new-account
Reddit billboarding, pretending you "found" the app, vote manipulation,
celebrating reach. Reuse structure, never execution.
