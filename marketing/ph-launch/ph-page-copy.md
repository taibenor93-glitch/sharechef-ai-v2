## Tagline (51 chars)

## Description
It's 6:45 PM. The fridge is full and you still don't know what's for dinner.

ShareChef AI ends that moment. Tell Micheli, your personal AI chef, what you have: by voice, or by typing three ingredients. She builds a real dinner from exactly that, then talks you through the cooking out loud, step by step. Your hands stay busy, your screen stays clean.

She speaks every language. She never asks you to buy anything. And the food you already paid for stops going in the trash.

Built by one founder over ten months, for working parents, students, and anyone who opens a fridge with no plan. Free on iPhone to start.
