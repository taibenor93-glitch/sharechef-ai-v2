# The ShareChef Marketing Doctrine
### (Tai's own words, distilled — 2026-08-14. Read this before writing ANY post, ad, or pitch.)

## The insight that started it
"I pay for AI because it solved my problems — Fable is my IT, GPT became my attorney, my doctor.
Nobody sold me anything. They showed me time saved, money saved, problems solved. People will pay to save."

## The diagnosis
Nothing moved on social because we were selling the QUARTER — "look, an AI chef, she talks, she speaks French."
Nobody wakes up wanting an AI chef. People wake up with problems.
"I cook with a chef — big deal. What do I bring to the table?" → Until the answer is a SOLVED PROBLEM, nobody cares.

## The problem we solve (say it exactly like this)
It's 6 PM. You're tired. The fridge has rice, two eggs, half an onion.
Groceries cost a fortune. Delivery wants $38 plus tip for mediocre food.
Micheli turns the food you ALREADY PAID FOR into dinner. The $38 stays in your pocket.

**We are not selling cooking. We are selling found money and a solved evening.**
Selling a quarter for a dollar? Better — we sell people their own dollar back.

## The audiences
1. Working mothers — kids eat real food, money stays in the family, no 6 PM battle.
2. College kids in dorms — rice and one tomato IS dinner. Broke ≠ hungry.

## The rules (never break)
- Problem first. Demo as proof. Savings as the close.
- Show the math on screen: "Delivery $38 → This $0."
- The demo is REAL: the actual app, Micheli's actual voice, real ingredients. Never fake a demo.
- No "AI-powered," no "revolutionary," no "game-changer," no tech jargon. The math does the bragging.
- Like OpenAI/Anthropic launches: show the product doing the thing, live, uncut — let the viewer sell themselves.
- Every single post = problem solving, time saving, money saving. That is the whole goal.

## The daily format ($0 Dinner)
"I HAD: [2-3 humble ingredients]" → the real app makes the dish → "THE MATH: Delivery ~$X → This $0 · N minutes · serves N."
Same skeleton every day, different ingredients. Post at ~5 PM — the exact hour people stare at the fridge and open DoorDash.

## Build in public (the other lane)
Founder story posts with real numbers and real milestones — like the launch posts that win on Product Hunt.
People engage with journeys, not ads. "Solo founder. Ten months. One kitchen at a time."
